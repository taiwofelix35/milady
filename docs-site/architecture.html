How It Works

Think of Milady On Tempo as one connected user journey from launch to mint to active marketplace trading.

Step 1: Launch

Creators publish a collection so people can find it in one place.

Step 2: Mint

Collectors mint during active phases with clear price and limits.

Step 3: Trade

People buy, list, and make offers without switching between many tools.

Step 4: Track

Profile and collection views show updates on your assets and market activity.

Step 5: Repeat

As market conditions change, users buy, list, and offer again using the same simple flow.

What Users Actually Do In This Flow

  1. Discover: find collections and items through Explore and Search.
  2. Decide: compare listed items, pricing, and activity context.
  3. Act: buy instantly, list owned NFTs, or place offers.
  4. Verify: watch profile and activity to confirm each action.
  5. Manage: update listings, accept offers, or continue collecting.

Why This Helps Users

  • Less confusion: everything is in one product flow.
  • Faster decisions: listings and activity are easy to scan.
  • Cleaner ownership view: profile tabs separate owned, listed, and offers.
