Marketplace Guide

This page explains every major action in the marketplace, from first visit to daily trading.

Before You Start

  • Use an official marketplace link.
  • Connect a wallet that supports Tempo.
  • Keep enough balance for your buy or offer amount.
  • Double-check collection name and token page before signing.

How To Use The Marketplace: Complete Walkthrough

1) Connect Wallet

  1. Click Connect Wallet in the top area of the app.
  2. Select your wallet and approve connection.
  3. Confirm your address appears in the navigation/profile area.

2) Discover Collections And NFTs

  1. Open Explore for broad discovery.
  2. Use Search for specific token IDs, wallet addresses, or collections.
  3. Open collection pages to see listed count, floor direction, and recent activity.

3) Buy Now

  1. Open an NFT card or detail page with a live listing.
  2. Press Buy Now.
  3. Review the final price shown in wallet.
  4. Confirm transaction.
  5. After confirmation, check Profile then Owned.

4) List Your NFT

  1. Go to Profile then Owned and open the NFT you want to sell.
  2. Press List.
  3. Enter your listing price and confirm.
  4. Sign in wallet.
  5. Your NFT should appear under Profile then Listed and in marketplace results.

5) Edit Or Cancel Your Listing

  1. Open Profile then Listed.
  2. Select the listed NFT.
  3. Choose cancel listing or re-list at a new price.
  4. Confirm in wallet.

6) Make An Offer

  1. Open an NFT page.
  2. Choose Make Offer.
  3. Enter amount and confirm.
  4. Track offer status in Profile then Offers.

7) Accept An Offer (As Seller)

  1. Open the NFT you own.
  2. Review incoming offers.
  3. Accept the offer you want.
  4. Confirm in wallet.
  5. Check Profile and Activity after confirmation.

Where To Track Everything

  • Profile: Owned, listed, offers, and your activity.
  • Collection page: Floor, listed count, and recent events.
  • NFT page: Current listing, offer actions, and ownership context.
  • Search: Find by token, wallet, or transaction references.

Safety Checklist

  • Verify collection and token before every buy, offer, or listing action.
  • Read wallet confirmation details before signing.
  • Ignore direct messages with unofficial links.
  • If a transaction fails, do not spam retries. Wait, refresh, and try once more.

If Something Looks Wrong

  • Refresh the page and check Profile and Activity tabs again.
  • Confirm wallet is still connected to the expected address.
  • Use Search with token ID or your address to find the latest state.
  • See Help Topics page for quick fixes.
