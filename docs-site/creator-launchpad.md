# Creator Launchpad Guide

This is the markdown source for the Creator Launchpad Guide. The HTML version is now live as launchpad.html.

## Steps

1. **Prepare Your Collection**
   - Artwork: Prepare all images and metadata (name, description, traits).
   - Supply: Decide total number of NFTs.
   - Mint Price: Set your price in MILADY.
   - Allowlist (optional): Prepare wallet addresses for early access.

2. **Launchpad Setup**
   - Go to the Launchpad section in the app.
   - Connect your creator wallet.
   - Click "Create Collection" and fill in all required fields.
   - Upload artwork and metadata as prompted.
   - Set mint phases (allowlist/public), price, and supply.
   - Review all details and confirm.

3. **Publish and Share**
   - Once your collection is live, copy the mint link.
   - Share with your community and on social channels.
   - Monitor mint progress in real time from your creator dashboard.

4. **After Mint**
   - Track secondary sales and offers in the marketplace.
   - Engage with your holders and keep them updated.
   - Update collection info if needed (profile image, description, etc.).

For advanced setup or troubleshooting, see Help Topics or contact support.
