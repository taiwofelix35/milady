Welcome

Milady On Tempo is a full NFT flow in one place: mint, discover, buy, list, make offers, and track everything from your profile.

Network: Tempo Supply: 1,111 Mint Price: 3 MILADY

Start Here (First 10 Minutes)

  1. Connect your wallet on the marketplace.
  2. Open Explore to browse live listings.
  3. Use Search to jump to a collection, wallet, or token.
  4. Open any NFT page and decide: Buy Now, List, or Offer.
  5. Check your Profile to confirm ownership and activity.

If you are new, read Marketplace Guide in full once. It covers every user action step-by-step.

For Collectors

  1. Open a collection page.
  2. Check mint phase and price.
  3. Connect wallet and mint.
  4. View your NFTs in your profile.

For Traders

  1. Use Explore or Search to find items.
  2. Open an NFT and choose Buy Now or Make Offer.
  3. List your NFT from your owned items.
  4. Track status in profile activity.

For Creators

  1. Create your collection in Launchpad.
  2. Set mint windows, price, and limits.
  3. Publish collection info.
  4. Share mint link with your community.
