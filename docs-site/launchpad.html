Creator Launchpad Guide

This page explains how to launch your own NFT collection on Milady On Tempo, from setup to mint.

1. Prepare Your Collection

  • Artwork: Prepare all images and metadata (name, description, traits).
  • Supply: Decide total number of NFTs.
  • Mint Price: Set your price in MILADY.
  • Allowlist (optional): Prepare wallet addresses for early access.

2. Launchpad Setup

  1. Go to the Launchpad section in the app.
  2. Connect your creator wallet.
  3. Click "Create Collection" and fill in all required fields.
  4. Upload artwork and metadata as prompted.
  5. Set mint phases (allowlist/public), price, and supply.
  6. Review all details and confirm.

3. Publish and Share

  1. Once your collection is live, copy the mint link.
  2. Share with your community and on social channels.
  3. Monitor mint progress in real time from your creator dashboard.

4. After Mint

  • Track secondary sales and offers in the marketplace.
  • Engage with your holders and keep them updated.
  • Update collection info if needed (profile image, description, etc.).
For advanced setup or troubleshooting, see Help Topics or contact support.
