Common Questions

What is the best order to use the app if I am new?

Connect wallet, browse Explore, open NFT detail pages, then start with one small Buy Now transaction before trying listing and offers.

Where do I buy listed NFTs?

Use Explore, Search, or collection pages, then tap Buy Now and confirm in wallet.

Where do I see what I own?

Open your profile and check the owned tab.

Where do I track my offers?

Profile includes offer and activity views so you can monitor changes quickly.

How do I list my NFT?

Open your NFT detail page, choose list, set a price, and confirm.

How do I cancel a listing?

Open your listed NFT from Profile then Listed, choose cancel listing, then confirm in wallet.

How do I change listing price?

Cancel old listing first, then create a new listing with your updated price.

Where do I see sale and offer history?

Check Activity in your profile and collection pages for the latest marketplace actions.

I bought an NFT but do not see it yet. What should I do?

Refresh the app, reopen your profile, and verify the same wallet address is connected.

I cannot click Buy Now or List. Why?

Usually wallet is disconnected, wrong wallet account is active, or the item state just changed. Refresh and try again.

My offer was not accepted. Is that an error?

No. Sellers choose whether to accept an offer. You can track offer status in your profile.

How can I avoid mistakes?

Always verify collection and token details on page and in wallet confirmation before signing.

What if something looks outdated?

Check the roadmap and mint guide pages first, then rely on official project channels.
