# Milady On Tempo Documentation

Milady On Tempo is an NFT product on Tempo for launching collections, minting, and trading in one place.

## What We Are Offering

Milady On Tempo offers a full user journey:

1. Creators launch collections and set mint conditions.
2. Collectors mint with clear supply and price details.
3. Traders discover, buy, list, and offer across collections.
4. Community members track wallet and collection activity.

## How To Use The Product

### For Collectors

1. Open the collection page.
2. Review mint details and phase status.
3. Connect wallet and mint during active phase.
4. After mint, manage your NFTs from profile and marketplace pages.

### For Traders

1. Use Explore and Search to find listings and activity.
2. Open NFT detail pages to buy now or place offers.
3. Use profile tabs to track owned, listed, offers, and recent activity.

### For Creators

1. Use Launchpad to publish your collection.
2. Set phase windows, pricing, and wallet limits.
3. Publish collection metadata for global discovery.
