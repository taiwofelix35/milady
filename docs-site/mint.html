How To Mint

  1. Open the official mint page from project channels.
  2. Connect your wallet.
  3. Check price and quantity before confirming.
  4. Submit transaction and wait for confirmation.
  5. Refresh your profile to see minted NFTs.

Always verify you are on the official mint page before signing.
